Welcome to WeJure! WeJure is a decentralized, peer-to-peer social network written in ClojureScript, developed at HKU Business School.

To get started, please read our [Background](pages-output/docs) and [Step by Step Guide](pages-output/example).

To know more about our design and implementation ideas, please read our [posts](archives).

## Features

- Accounts with a profile picture and a bio
- Posts with text and/or an image; a feed of the people you follow; deleting your own posts
- Following and unfollowing users
- Searching users by name and posts by text
- End-to-end encrypted direct messages, and blocking users
- Group chat with layered encryption (backend only so far)

## Report Bugs

WeJure is currently under active development.
If you find any bugs or errors, we would appreciate it if you could [report them on GitHub](https://github.com/clojure-finance/WeJure/issues) so that we can fix them.
